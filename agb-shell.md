# AGB shell measurements

Measured by hand (2026-11-04), not yet used by any tool (the shell models are in src/cartridge.py). All sizes in mm.

The "shoulder" is an extra bit sticking out to the sides right at the top. The body width should be the same as the
DMG and CGB cartridges (57).

## Overall case size

- height: 34.5
- width body: 57
- width shoulder: 60 (1.5 out on each side)
- shoulder height: 7

## Recess

- height: 23
- width: 44 (as the DMG's; centered → 6.5 in from the body's sides)

Not measured: how far the recess is from the top or bottom.

## Label (hard to measure accurately)

- height: 22
- width: 43

## Top grip bow

Same width as the recess.
It has 2 creases, each on a curve. Might be a circular arc. The 2 creases bow upwards and meet at the ends in sharp
points, each one straight above the recess side edge.
The bottom crease has its highest point in the horizontal center of the cart, 2.5 mm below the top outer edge of the
shell.
The top crease tops out at 2 mm from the top.

Not measured: how far down the creases' ends (the points) are.

## Logo

Between the 2 creases and the top of the recess is the "GAME BOY ADVANCE" logo. The logo is about 28 mm wide.
